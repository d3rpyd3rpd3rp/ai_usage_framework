Risks

What are the downsides of using AI in this context?

Minor Risks

  • AI might leave obvious traces of usage such as emoji spam or "AI-like speech"
  • Could be socially mocked for using AI on a trivial task

Major Risks

  • Consequences if confidential data were to be leaked via AI training
  • Overreliance on AI could cause productivity issues if WiFI or AI servers went down
